Problem Statement:

Cyclists often struggle with a fragmented digital experience, relying on separate, disjointed platforms to discover safe riding routes, research authentic gear reviews, track performance metrics, and organise community group rides. This lack of a unified ecosystem leaves rides- from beginners to seasoned athletes- facing inconsistent route safety data, biased product recommendations, and difficulty connecting with local cycling clubs and events. Gearshift addresses this fragmentation by consolidating route exploration, gear insights, performance tracking, and local community networking into a single, cohesive platform designed to make cycling more accessible, informed and socially engaging. 

Project Description:

A platform where cyclists discover routes, review gear, track rides and connect with local cycling groups.
